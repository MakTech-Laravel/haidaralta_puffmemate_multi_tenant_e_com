# Profile

## Driver edit details is a compact two-card form
GET /driver/profile/edit is a dense field editor under DriverLayout: avatar (~88px) beside name/phone/email in one You card, license/type/address/vehicle photo in one License & vehicle card. Do not restore a full-width Photo island, Back to profile, Cancel, or a third Contact card. One Save (header on sm+, full-width at the bottom on xs). Profile tab is the back path. Keep the form max-w-2xl so it does not stretch empty on wide chrome.
