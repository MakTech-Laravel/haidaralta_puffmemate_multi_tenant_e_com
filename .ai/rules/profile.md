# Profile

## Driver edit details mirrors the profile page
GET /driver/profile/edit is an editable version of the Profile tab under DriverLayout: accent identity strip (initials avatar + camera overlay, heading name, Save), a Contact 2-col icon field grid, then a Vehicle image well with camera overlay and type select. Do not restore dashed FileUpload dropzones, a You card, a License & vehicle split, Back to profile, Cancel, or a max-w-2xl form. Profile tab is the back path.
