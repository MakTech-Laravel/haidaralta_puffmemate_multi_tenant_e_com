# Platformbranch

## Platform and branch nav are dropdown groups
Platform/branch sidebars are one unlabeled NavSection: Dashboard (and other singles) as navLink, multi-item clusters as navGroup dropdowns. Do not add a SidebarGroupLabel over a dropdown of the same name. Soon rows use navSoon (disabled, no href). Do not add pages or routes from the sidebar. Keep Horizon/Pulse/backups/SMTP/FCM/realtime off the branch tree.
