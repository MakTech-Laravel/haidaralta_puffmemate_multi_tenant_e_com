# Platformbranch Orders

## Order list badges use option colors
Platform and branch order lists color Source, Status, and Payment badges from the option value: pending/pending collection amber, processing sky, ready cyan, assigned indigo, picked up violet, out for delivery blue, delivery failed and payment failed rose, returning orange, delivered and collected emerald, cancelled zinc, customer sky, driver sale violet, wholesale and verified teal, refunded violet. Keep enum labels. Do not return every badge to outline or secondary.
