# Route Js Components Driver

## Driver fifth tab is Route not Chat
The fifth driver field tab is Route (center): GET /driver/route, RouteController::index, sequenced remaining then completed stops from DemoCatalog. Do not restore a Chats tab. Activity stays sidebar/account only.

## Activity is a driver field tab
Route stays the center tab. Activity is the fourth tab, not sidebar-only. Do not restore a Chats tab or an Alerts footer tab.

## Earnings is a driver field tab
Route stays the center tab. Earnings is the fourth field tab at GET /driver/earnings. Activity stays account-only. Do not restore a Chats, Activity, or Alerts footer tab.
