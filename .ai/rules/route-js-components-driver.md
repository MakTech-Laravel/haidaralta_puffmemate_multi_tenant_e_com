# Route Js Components Driver

## Driver center tab is Route not Chat
The center driver field tab is Route: GET /driver/route, RouteController::index, sequenced remaining then completed stops from DemoCatalog. Do not restore a Chats tab. Activity stays account-sheet only.

## Earnings and Place order are field tabs
Route stays the center tab. Earnings is the fourth field tab at GET /driver/earnings. The fifth tab is Place order at GET /driver/place-order. Activity stays in the header sheet. Do not restore a Chats, Activity, Account, Profile, Old orders, or Alerts footer tab.
